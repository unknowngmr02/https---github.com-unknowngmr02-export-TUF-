From the ground, the angle of elevation to the top of a tower is 60°. A person moves 20 m up the same vertical line, and now the angle becomes 30°. Find the height of the tower.
Q
Angle 60° from ground, 30° after rising 20 m - find the tower height.
Given
Angle (ground) = 60°, Angle (+20 m) = 30°
Horizontal distance d stays the same
Remaining height = h − 20
Step 1
tan 60° = hd
tan 60° = √3
√3 = hd
d = h√3
Step 2
Remaining height = h − 20
tan 30° = h − 20d
tan 30° = 1√3
1√3 = h − 20h/√3
Step 3
2h = 60
h = 30
Height of tower = 30 m
