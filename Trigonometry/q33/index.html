Trigonometry
From a window 10 m above the ground, the angle of elevation of a tower top is 30° and the angle of depression of its foot is 45°. Find the tower height.
Q
Window 10 m up, elevation 30° to top, depression 45° to foot. Find tower height.
Given
Window height = 10 m
∠ elevation = 30°, ∠ depression = 45°
Find: total height of tower
Step 1
For the triangle at the tower's foot:
tan 45° = OppositeAdjacent
Opposite = 10 m
Adjacent = x
1 = 10x
x = 10 m
Step 2
We know x = 10 m
Now find extra height h above the window
tan 30° = h10
tan 30° = 1√3
1√3 = h10
h = 10√3 m
Step 3
Careful:
h is NOT the total height!
It's only the part above the window
Window itself = 10 m above ground
Total height = 10 + 10√3 m
G
Window height = 10 m
∠ elevation = 30°, ∠ depression = 45°
Find: total tower height
1
tan 45° = Opposite/Adjacent
10/x = 1 ⇒ x = 10 m
2
tan 30° = h/10 = 1/√3
h = 10/√3 m
3
Total = window + h
10 + 10/√3 m

B10√3 m
C20 m
D10 + 10√3 m
