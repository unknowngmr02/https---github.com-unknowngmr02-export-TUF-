A drone is observed from two points on opposite sides of its foot at angles 45° and 60°. The two points are 100 m apart. Find the drone height.
Q
Drone seen from two sides at 45°, 60°; points 100 m apart. Find drone height.
Given
Height of drone = h
Distances from foot = x, y
x + y = 100 m
Step 1
tan 45° = hx
tan 45° = 1
h = x
Step 2
tan 60° = hy
tan 60° = √3
y = h√3
Step 3
Both x, y now in terms of h
x + y = 100
h + h√3 = 100
h ( 1 + 1√3 ) = 100
h = 50(3 - √3) m
