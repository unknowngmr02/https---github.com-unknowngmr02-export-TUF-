Now, which trigonometric ratio uses the opposite and adjacent sides?

It's ten.

So,

ten theta = Opposite / Adjacent

Substituting the values,

ten theta = 7 / 7 square root of 3

The 7 cancels out, giving us,

ten theta = 1 / square root of 3
