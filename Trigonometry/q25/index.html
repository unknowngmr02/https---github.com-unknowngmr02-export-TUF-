Q
In a 3-4-5 triangle, tan θ = 3/4. Find sin θ + cos θ.
Given
Opposite side = 3
Adjacent side = 4
Hypotenuse = 5
Find: sin θ + cos θ
Step 1
sin θ = OppositeHypotenuse
sin θ = 35
Step 2
cos θ = AdjacentHypotenuse
cos θ = 45
Step 3
sin θ + cos θ
= 35 + 45
Same denominator, add numerators
= 75
