Now in this problem, we have a vertical stick.

The question says that the shadow of the stick is equal to its height.

And we need to find the sun's angle of elevation.

Let the angle of elevation be "thee-ta".

Before solving, let us quickly understand the picture.

The stick is standing straight, so its height becomes the opposite side.

The shadow is on the ground, so it becomes the adjacent side.

That is all we need for this question.
<break time="0.5s" />

Since we know the opposite side and the adjacent side, the correct trigonometric ratio to use is "ten".

So, ten thee-ta is equal to Opposite upon Adjacent.

Now let us put the given values.

The height is equal to the shadow.

So, ten thee-ta is equal to Height upon Shadow, which becomes 1 upon 1.

That means tan thee-ta is equal to 1.
<break time="0.2s" />

Now we know one standard trigonometric value.

ten 45 degree is equal to 1.

Since ten thee-ta is also equal to 1, the value of thee-ta must be 45 degrees.

Got it? Perfect.
<break time="0.6s" />

So the sun's angle of elevation is "45 degrees".

Hence, the correct option is *Option A, 45 degrees*.
