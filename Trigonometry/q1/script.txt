Now in this problem, we are given a right triangle.

The side opposite to angle A is 9 senti-mee-ter, and the side adjacent to angle A is 12 senti-mee-ter.

The question is asking us to find the value of "tan A".
<break time="0.4s" />

Now what does "tan" actually mean?

For any right triangle,

tan A is equal to Opposite Side divided by Adjacent Side.
<break time="0.4s" />

Now let us put the given values into the formula.

tan A = 9 divided by 12.

Both 9 and 12 are divisible by 3.

After simplifying, we get,

tan A = 3 by 4.

Done.
<break time="0.2s" />

So the final answer will be "3 by 4".

Hence, the correct option is "Option A".
