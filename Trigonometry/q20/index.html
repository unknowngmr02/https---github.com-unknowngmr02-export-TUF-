Q
A person's eye level is 1.5 m above the ground. From 18 m away, the angle of elevation to the top of a pole is 45 degrees. Find the pole height.
Given
Distance from pole = 18 m
Eye level height = 1.5 m
Angle of elevation = 45°
Find: full height of pole
Step 1
Adjacent side = 18 m
Opposite side = X m
tan θ = OppositeAdjacent
tan 45° = X18
tan 45° = 1
X = 18 m
Step 2
Height above eye level = 18 m
But full height is asked!
Eye level = 1.5 m
Full height = 18 + 1.5
= 19.5 m
