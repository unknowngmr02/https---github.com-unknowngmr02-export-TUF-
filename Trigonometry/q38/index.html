Two towers are 30 m apart. From the foot of the first, the angle of elevation of the top of the second is 60 degrees; from the foot of the second, the angle of elevation of the top of the first is 30 degrees. Find the difference in their heights.
Q
Two towers, 30 m apart - find the difference in their heights.
Given
Distance between towers = 30 m
Angle at foot of Tower 1 = 60°
Angle at foot of Tower 2 = 30°
Step 1
Tower 1 gives us a 60° angle directly, so find Tower 2's height first
tan θ = HeightBase
tan 60° = H₂30
tan 60° = √3
H₂ = 30√3 m
Step 2
Now use the same idea for Tower 1's 30° angle
tan 30° = H₁30
tan 30° = 1√3
H₁ = 30√3
H₁ = 10√3 m
Step 3
Tower 2 = 30√3 m
Tower 1 = 10√3 m
Difference = Tower 2 − Tower 1
30√3 − 10√3
= 20√3 m
