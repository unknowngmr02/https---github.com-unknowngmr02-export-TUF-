Q
tan θ + cot θ = 10/3. Find sin θ · cos θ.
Given
tan θ = PB
cot θ = BP
Step 1
PB + BP = 103
LCM
P² + B²PB = 103
Step 2
Pythagoras
P² + B² = H²
H²PB = 103
Step 3
sin θ = PH
cos θ = BH
sin θ · cos θ = PBH²
Step 4
H²PB = 103
Reciprocal PBH² = 310
= sin θ · cos θ
sin θ · cos θ = 310
