Q
Two points, 100 m apart, lie on the same side of a balloon. The angles of elevation from the two points are 60° and 30°. Find the height of the balloon.
Given
Distance between points = 100 m
Near-point angle = 60°
Far-point angle = 30°
Let nearer distance = x m
Farther distance = x + 100 m
Step 1
tan θ = heightbase
tan 60° = hx
√3 = hx
h = x√3
Step 2
tan 30° = hx + 100
1/√3 = hx + 100
h = x + 100√3
Step 3
Both = same height, so equate:
x√3 = x + 100√3
Multiply both sides by √3:
3x = x + 100
2x = 100
x = 50
Step 4
Height h needed, not x
h = x√3
h = 50√3 m
