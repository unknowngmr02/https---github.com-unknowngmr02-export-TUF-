Now in this problem, we are given an acute angle.

For this angle, the opposite side is 8.
And the high-pot-en-use is 17.

The question is asking us to find its ten.

For ten theta, we need opposite by adjacent.
Opposite is already given, but adjacent is not given.
So first, we have to find the adjacent side.
<break time="0.5s" />

Let the adjacent side be x.

Using Pythagoras theorem,

high-pot-en-use square is equal to opposite square pluss adjacent square.

So,

17 square is equal to 8 square pluss x square.

That means,

289 is equal to 64 pluss x square.

So x square is equal to 289 - 64.

After subtracting, x square is equal to 225.
So x is equal to 15.
<break time="0.2s" />

Now we can find ten theta.

ten theta is equal to opposite by adjacent.

So ten theta is equal to 8 by 15.
<break time="0.6s" />

Hence, the ten of the angle is "8 by 15".
