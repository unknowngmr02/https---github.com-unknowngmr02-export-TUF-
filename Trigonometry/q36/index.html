A building is 12 m tall. From its top, the angle of depression to the base of a tower is 60°, and the angle of elevation to the top of the tower is 30°. Find the height of the tower.
Q
Building 12 m; depression 60°, elevation 30°. Find tower height.
Given
Let horizontal distance = x
(same x in both triangles)
Step 1
Building = 12 m
Horizontal distance = x
∠dep = ∠elev (alternate angles)
tan 60° = 12x = √3
x = 12√3 = 4√3 m
Step 2
Let extra height above building = h
Using tan 30°
tan 30° = h4√3 = 1√3
h = 4 m
h = only extra height above building
Step 3
Building = 12 m already
Tower extra = 4 m above
Tower height = 12 + 4 = 16 m
G
Distance between building & tower = x
1
tan 60° = 12x = √3
x = 4√3 m
2
tan 30° = h4√3 = 1√3
h = 4 m
3
Tower height = 12 + 4 = 16 m
