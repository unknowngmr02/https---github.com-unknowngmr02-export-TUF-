In this problem, a road rises 10 mee-ter for every 10 root 3 mee-ter of horizontal distance.


What does this mean in simple words?


It means if we draw the road and the ground, they form a right triangle.


The upward rise becomes the height, which is 10 mee-ter.


And The horizontal distance becomes the base, which is 10 root 3 mee-ter.


And the problem is asking us to find the angle made by the road with the ground.


So let us call this angle theta.


<break time="0.5s" />


Here we know two things already.


We know the height.


And we know the base.


Whenever height and base are given, the trigonometric ratio that connects them is "tan theta".




As ten theta equals Height upon Base


So,


ten theta equals 10 upon 10 root 3


After simplifying this, the 10 gets cancelled.


This becomes,


ten theta equals 1 upon root 3

ten 30 degree is equal to 1 upon root 3.
