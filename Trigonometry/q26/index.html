Q
A person walks 20 m toward a tower. The angle of elevation changes from 30 degrees to 45 degrees. Find the tower height.
Given
Angle (far) = 30°
Angle (near) = 45°
Walked = 20 m
Find: height of tower
Step 1
Opposite = h, Adjacent = x
tan θ = heightbase
tan 45° = hx
tan 45° = 1
h = x
Step 2
tan 30° = hx + 20
tan 30° = 1√3
Since h = x, replace x with h
1√3 = hh + 20
Cross-multiplying: h + 20 = √3 h
20 = h(√3 − 1)
h = 20√3 − 1
Step 3
Rationalize the denominator
h = 20√3 − 1 × √3 + 1√3 + 1
h = 10(√3 + 1) m
