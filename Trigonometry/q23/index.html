From a point 30 m away, the angles of elevation of the top of a building and the top of a flagstaff on it are 30 degrees and 60 degrees. Find the flagstaff height.
Q
A point is 30 m from a building. Angles of elevation to the top of the building and the top of a flagstaff on it are 30° and 60°.
Given
Distance = 30 m
Angle to building top = 30°
Angle to flagstaff top = 60°
Find: flagstaff height (x)
Step 1
For the building:
tan 30° = h30
h = 30√3
h = 10√3 m
Step 2
For the flagstaff:
tan 60° = h + x30
h + x = 30√3 m
(this is the total height)
Step 3
Flagstaff height only:
x = Total - Building
x = 30√3 - 10√3
x = 20√3 m
