For an acute angle θ, sinθ + cosθ = 7/5. Find sinθ · cosθ.
Q
Acute angle θ: sinθ + cosθ = 7/5. Find sinθ · cosθ.
Given
sinθ + cosθ = 7/5
Find: sinθ · cosθ
Trick: square both sides
Step 1
(sinθ + cosθ)² = (7/5)²
After opening the bracket:
sin²θ + cos²θ + 2sinθcosθ = 4925
Step 2
Identity:
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 (any θ)
Substitute into Step 1:
1 + 2sinθcosθ = 4925
Need: sinθ · cosθ
Step 3
Isolate 2sinθcosθ:
Subtract 1:
2sinθcosθ = 2425
Divide by 2:
sinθ · cosθ = 1225

sinθ + cosθ = 7/5
