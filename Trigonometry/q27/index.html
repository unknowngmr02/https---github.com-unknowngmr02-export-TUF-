Two observation points on opposite sides of a tower are 60 m apart. Their angles of elevation are 30° and 45°. Find the height of the tower.
Q
Two points, 60 m apart, opposite sides of tower. Angles: 30° and 45°.
Given
h = height of the tower
x = distance of 30° point
60 - x = distance of 45° point
Step 1
tan 45° = heightdistance
tan 45° = 1
1 = h60 - x
h = 60 - x
Step 2
tan 30° = 1√3
1√3 = hx
x = √3 h
Step 3
h = 60 - x
60 = h(√3 + 1)
h = 60√3 + 1
h = 30(√3 - 1) m
G
