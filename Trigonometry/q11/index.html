If tan theta = 7/24 for an acute angle, find sec theta.
Q
Find: sec θ
Using the tan ratio
tan θ = PerpendicularBase = 724
Do these have to be the exact side lengths? No - it's just a ratio! Any sides in ratio 7:24 work. 7 & 24 just keep the calculation easy.
Acute angle theta: tan theta = 7/24. Find sec theta.
tan θ = 7/24
Given
We know two sides of the triangle
but sec θ needs Hypotenuse & Base
Perpendicular = 7 units, Base = 24 units
Step 1
First, let's find the hypotenuse
Hyp = sqrt(Perp² + Base²)
Hyp = sqrt(7² + 24²)
= sqrt(49 + 576) = sqrt(625)
Hyp = 25
Step 2
sec θ = HypotenuseBase
sec θ = 2524
θ acute, so sec θ is positive
