13 m ladder, 12 m window height. Find distance of foot from wall.
Given
Ladder = 13 m
Wall height = 12 m
Step 1
Hypotenuse² = Base² + Perpendicular²
13² = x² + 12²
Step 2
169 = x² + 144
Step 3
x² = 169 - 144
x² = 25
Step 4
x = √25 = 5 m
